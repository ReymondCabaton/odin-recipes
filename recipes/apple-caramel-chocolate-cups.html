Apple Caramel Chocolate Cups

DESCRIPTION

These apple caramel chocolate cups tuck gooey apple cider caramel inside a rich chocolate shell. Inspired by classic caramel apples, these bite-sized treats deliver sweet-tart flavor with an optional sprinkle of apple chips for extra crunch—all with just five ingredients.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1 cup unfiltered apple cider
  • 3/4 cup white sugar
  • 1/4 cup heavy whipping cream
  • 1 cup bittersweet chocolate chips
  • 1 cup semisweet chocolate chips
  • 2 teaspoons coconut oil (optional)
  • 1/4 cup chopped apple chips (optional)

STEPS

  1. Stir together apple cider and sugar in a saucepan and bring to a boil over medium heat. Reduce heat to medium-low. Simmer, stirring occasionally, until thickened and a candy thermometer registers 240 degrees F (115 degrees C), about 15 minutes.
  2. Stir in cream and continue cooking over medium-low, stirring occasionally, until thickened and a candy thermometer registers 240 degrees F (115 degrees C), about 5 minutes. Remove from heat and pour into a medium heatproof bowl. Let sit at room temperature to cool slightly until easy to handle, about 45 minutes. You should have a heaping 1/2 cup.
  3. Meanwhile, line a 24-cup mini muffin pan with paper liners. Microwave bittersweet chocolate, semisweet chocolate, and coconut oil (if using) in a medium microwave-safe bowl on high power for 30 seconds. Stir, and continue heating in 30-second increments, stirring after each time, until melted, 1 minute to 1 minute 30 seconds total.
  4. Spoon 1 teaspoon of chocolate mixture into the bottom of each liner. Use a measuring spoon to spread chocolate across the bottom and partially up the sides. Freeze, uncovered, until the chocolate is just set, about 5 minutes.
  5. Spoon about 1 heaping teaspoon of apple caramel into the center of each chocolate layer. Freeze, uncovered, until apple caramel is just set, about 5 minutes. Spoon remaining chocolate mixture over apple caramel (about 1 teaspoon per cup), spreading as needed to completely cover. Sprinkle evenly with chopped apple chips. Freeze until completely firm, about 5 minutes. Remove apple caramel cups from pan and serve.
