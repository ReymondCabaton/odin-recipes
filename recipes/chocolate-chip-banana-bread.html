Chocolate Chip Banana Bread

DESCRIPTION

Chocolate Chip Banana Bread is a soft, moist, and flavorful baked treat made with ripe bananas and sweet chocolate chips. It combines the natural sweetness of bananas with rich chocolate in every bite, making it a perfect snack or dessert for any occasion.

    INGREDIENTS

  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup Land O Lakes® Butter, softened
  • 2 large Land O Lakes® Eggs
  • 2 medium (1 cup) ripe bananas, mashed
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup semi-sweet chocolate chips
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt

STEPS

  1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees F. Grease and flour 3 (5 3/4 x 3-inch) mini loaf pans.
  2. Beat sugar and butter in a large mixing bowl with an electric mixer on medium speed, scraping down the bowl often, until creamy.
  3. Add eggs and continue beating until well combined.
  4. Add bananas and vanilla and mix on low until combined.
  5. Stir in flour, chocolate chips, baking soda, and salt.
  6. Spoon batter into the prepared pans.
  7. Bake until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean, 33 to 38 minutes.
  8. Let cool for 5 minutes, remove the bread from the pans, and cool completely.
