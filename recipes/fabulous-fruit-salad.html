Fabulous Fruit Salad

DESCRIPTION

Fabulous Fruit Salad is a refreshing and delicious combination of fresh apples, nectarines, celery, dried cranberries, and crunchy walnuts mixed with creamy lemon yogurt. It offers a perfect balance of sweetness, freshness, and texture, making it a great snack or dessert for any occasion.

    INGREDIENTS

  • 1 red apple, cored and chopped
  • 1 Granny Smith apple, cored and chopped
  • 1 nectarine, pitted and sliced
  • 2 stalks celery, chopped
  • ½ cup dried cranberries
  • ½ cup chopped walnuts
  • 1 (8 ounce) container nonfat lemon yogurt

STEPS

  1. In a large bowl, combine red apple, Granny Smith apple, nectarine, celery, dried cranberries, and walnuts.
  2. Mix in yogurt.
  3. Chill until ready to serve.
